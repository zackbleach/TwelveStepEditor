# Privacy policy: 12 Step Editor for iOS

_Last updated: 25 October 2026_

The app collects no personal data, and there are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

- **Your presets** are stored in a folder you choose: on your device, or a cloud folder such as iCloud Drive that you pick in the Files app. The app reads and writes only the 12 Step Editor's preset files in that folder.
- **The 12 Step:** the app exchanges MIDI messages only with the 12 Step connected over USB.
- **Settings:** the app remembers which preset folder you chose. That stays on your device.
- **Network:** the app makes no network requests itself. Links on the About screen open in your browser.

Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
